Kallaway Reminders
🎯 4-Second Rule
Your best shot MUST land between 0 and 2 seconds. TikTok cuts distribution at 4s if the visual hook doesn't hold.
👁️ Visual hook = 100× verbal
No voiceover means frame 0 is your only hook. Shock shot or unexpected movement — never the classic intro wide.
🔄 Value loop
Structure: Context → Value hit → Context → Value hit. Front-load: deliver value in the first 3 seconds.
🎨 LUT = visual identity
A consistent LUT builds recognition. Your audience knows your universe before they even read your name. Consistency > technical quality.

4 Hook Tests (Kallaway)
Zero delay — Is the promise visible in <1 second?
Zero confusion — Would a 10-year-old understand?
Relevance (You/Your) — Is the pain/benefit before the technique?
Contrast — Is there a twist or narrative tension?
